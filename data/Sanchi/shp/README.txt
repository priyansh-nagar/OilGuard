Two shapefiles here: (1) 'tanker_location_from_viirs.shp' includes all tanker location points that derived from VIIRS Nightfire products and the reported tanker sinking location. The properties of the VIIRS Nightfire fire source area and temperature are included in the shapefiles properties. (2)'Oil_slick_delineations.shp' includes the two slicks that delineated from MSI images on 18 January 2018. The oil slick areas in unit of square km are included in the shapefile properties.
